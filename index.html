How to play

  • Pinch to draw

    Touch your thumb and index finger together to start the pen.

  • Open your fingers to stop

    Spread them apart, then wait a moment. Your doodle appears.

  • Keep it simple

    One big shape works best. Try a heart, circle, star, wave, swirl, triangle, square or zigzag.
