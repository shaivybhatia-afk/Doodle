How it works

  • Point to draw

    Raise just your index finger. Prefer pinching? Switch modes above the video.

  • Peace sign to lift

    Add a second finger to pause the pen, then hold an open palm for one second to finish.

  • Keep it simple

    One big, clear shape works best. Tap a card above to see how each one is drawn.
